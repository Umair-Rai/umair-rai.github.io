Overview

The platform automatically identifies a pet's breed from a photo across 139 cat and dog breeds, and wraps that model in a web application with pet-related services and an administrative portal.

It was my final year project at Bahria University and combined model research with a deployed, working system.

My contributions

  • Designed and implemented the full computer-vision pipeline.
  • Used YOLOv8m-Seg for pet detection and a Swin Transformer for breed classification.
  • Fine-tuned and compared ResNet-18 and Swin Transformer models.
  • Developed the FastAPI model-serving backend and deployed the inference service on Modal.
  • Integrated the trained models with the wider React and Node.js application.

Achievements

  • 91.31% Top-1 accuracy, 90.67% macro F1 and 99.15% Top-5 accuracy across 139 breeds.
  • Moved from a trained model to a deployed inference service, not just a notebook result.
  • Graduated with a 3.76/4.00 CGPA, highest in class, Cum Laude.
