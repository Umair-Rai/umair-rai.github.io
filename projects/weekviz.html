Overview

WeekViz is a workforce-management SaaS used by multiple organizations. It brings attendance, timesheets, payroll, leave management, subscriptions and project management into one platform, with each organization's data kept separate.

I contributed as part of the Horrizon AI team, across the backend, the administrative panel and the billing system.

My contributions

  • Built the administrative panel.
  • Implemented Stripe subscription billing, including pro-rated seat-based pricing and subscription changes.
  • Developed backend APIs, access-control workflows and data-management features that support multiple organizations and their employees.
  • Built interactive dashboards and data visualizations, and worked on AI-powered functionality.
  • Supported production deployment and ongoing application maintenance.

Achievements

  • Implemented pro-rated, seat-based billing that handles subscription changes.
  • Multi-tenant access control so each organization only sees its own people and data.
  • Platform running in production at weekviz.com.
