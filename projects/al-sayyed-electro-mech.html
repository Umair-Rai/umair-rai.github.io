Overview

Al Sayyed supplies pump parts, mechanical seals and related industrial components. The site presents the product range in a structured catalog and lets business customers send inquiries rather than check out like a consumer shop.

I built it as a client project at Softrick Solutions, covering both the interface and the backend.

My contributions

  • Built the English and Arabic experience with a proper right-to-left layout.
  • Developed the product catalog for pump and mechanical seal components.
  • Implemented the inquiry-based cart and email notifications.
  • Built authentication and admin functionality.
  • Developed REST APIs and database integrations, and worked on application performance.

Achievements

  • Delivered a responsive, product-focused interface now live at eme6.com.
  • Full Arabic support, including a right-to-left layout.
  • Inquiry-based flow suited to B2B purchasing instead of a consumer checkout.
